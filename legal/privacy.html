Privacy Policy — flaww

Last updated: 16 August 2026 · Operated by PicoFlow (picoflow.io)

flaww is a private, single-operator social listening tool. It reads publicly posted content on Reddit, Threads, X, and LinkedIn, flags posts that are relevant to PicoFlow, and lets the operator reply in their own words. It is not a consumer product: there are no user accounts, no sign-up, and no way for a third party to use it.

1. Who we are

flaww is operated by PicoFlow ("we", "us"), the maker of the systematic review assistant at picoflow.io. For anything in this policy, including requests about your data, contact privacy@picoflow.io.

2. What data flaww processes

flaww only ever reads content that its author published publicly on a platform, through that platform's official API. It does not scrape, it does not read private messages, it does not read non-public profile data, and it never attempts to access anything behind a login that isn't the operator's own account.

What flaww does not process

  • No cookies, trackers, analytics, pixels, or device fingerprinting — flaww has no public web interface.
  • No email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or payment details of post authors.
  • No private messages, follower lists, or content restricted to a platform's logged-in users.
  • No special-category data is sought. Posts are matched on research and workflow keywords, not on health, political, religious, or biometric characteristics.
  • No data is collected from anyone under 16 knowingly; flaww does not target minors and platform terms already restrict accounts by age.

3. Why we process it, and our legal basis

We process public posts to find conversations where PicoFlow is genuinely relevant, so that a human can decide whether to reply. Under the UK/EU GDPR our legal basis is legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): responding to public discussion of a problem our product solves is a normal and expected use of a public post, is limited to data the author chose to publish, and is balanced by the safeguards below.

  • Only public posts matching a narrow, topic-specific keyword list are stored.
  • Replies are written by a human and posted verbatim; nothing is generated and sent on our behalf.
  • Volume caps, per-author cooldowns, and per-community limits prevent repeated contact with the same person.
  • No profiles are built for advertising, no data is enriched with outside sources, and nothing is used to score or target individuals.

Where a platform's own terms require consent for a given surface (for example, replying to comments on our own LinkedIn posts), that interaction happens only within the permissions the platform grants our app.

4. How long we keep it

  • Raw API payloads: automatically erased 30 days after ingestion.
  • Post text, public handle, and relevance assessment: retained while flaww is in operation, as the record of what was reviewed and why. Rejected posts are kept too, because they document what we chose not to reply to.
  • Operator replies: retained indefinitely as a record of the operator's own writing.
  • Operational counters, cursors, and locks: pruned continuously; they contain no personal data.

If you ask us to delete data about you (§7), we delete it regardless of these periods.

6. Security

  • Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database with TLS-only connections.
  • API credentials are held in Google Secret Manager, never in source control.
  • The service runs as a single private instance; access is limited to the operator.
  • The source code is public, but no data, credentials, or configuration secrets are published with it.

7. Your rights

If flaww has stored one of your public posts, you have the right to ask for a copy of that data, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to restrict or object to our processing, and to object to legitimate-interest processing at any time. There is no automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects.

Email privacy@picoflow.io with the link to the post or your handle on the platform. We will respond within 30 days. An objection or erasure request is honoured in full: we delete the stored records and add your handle to a permanent exclusion list so flaww does not surface your posts again.

If you are in the UK or EEA and are unhappy with our response, you may complain to your national data protection authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.

8. Deleted and edited posts

flaww takes a copy of a post at the moment it is read, so a post you later delete or edit on the platform may still exist in our database. Tell us and we will remove it. Records are also removed when the underlying post can no longer be retrieved during normal operation.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page if what flaww processes changes, and the date at the top will change with it. Material changes to the categories of data or the processors involved will be reflected here before the change takes effect.
